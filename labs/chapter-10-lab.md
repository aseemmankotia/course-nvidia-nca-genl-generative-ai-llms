# Chapter 10 Lab: Full NCA-GENL Exam Simulation and Test-Taking Strategy

**Estimated time:** 90 minutes

## Exercise

Take a full 50-question exam simulation under timed conditions (60 minutes); after time expires, review flagged questions; go through every question, read the explanation for correct and incorrect answers; identify patterns in your mistakes (e.g. always confusing RLHF vs instruction tuning); create a final review flashcard set for weak areas

## Key takeaway

The NCA-GENL exam is 50 questions in 60 minutes—manage time by flagging hard questions, eliminating wrong answers, and reviewing systematically; trust your preparation, stay calm, and answer every question.
